# Gallery Guidelines

## Purpose

Arjun Art Gallery is a long-term digital archive of creative work.

The gallery should preserve:

- finished artwork;
- selected work-in-progress photographs;
- the story or occasion behind the work;
- medium and year;
- a small number of meaningful creator-with-artwork photographs where appropriate.

## Curation principles

1. Preserve the character of the original artwork.
2. Image cleanup may correct crop, rotation, exposure, sharpness and distracting background objects.
3. Do not redraw, beautify or materially alter the artwork itself.
4. Do not alter faces or personal appearance in documentary photographs.
5. Prefer a few strong photographs over many near-duplicates.
6. Organize works by year, then collection or occasion.
7. Keep captions short and factual.
8. Preserve original high-resolution images outside the web-optimized copies whenever practical.

## Privacy

This is a public repository and website. Avoid publishing private school information, addresses, contact details, IDs, or other unnecessary personal information.
