# Arjun Art Gallery

A digital gallery preserving Arjun's drawings, paintings, crafts, sculptures and creative journey.

## Purpose

This repository is both:

- the long-term archive and source of truth for the gallery; and
- the source for the public GitHub Pages website.

The goal is to preserve not only finished artwork, but also selected making-process photographs and the story behind each piece.

## Curation principles

This gallery is intended as a long-term creative archive, not just a showcase of finished pieces.

It preserves:

- finished artworks;
- selected work-in-progress images;
- process and making moments;
- short factual captions;
- the year and collection context; and
- occasional artist-with-artwork photographs where meaningful.

### Image integrity

Image cleanup may improve crop, alignment, exposure and sharpness or remove distracting background objects, but should not materially change the original artwork or alter faces in documentary photographs.

Some works are intentionally archived exactly as supplied, without cleanup, when preservation of the original photograph is preferred.

## Privacy

This is a public repository and website. Avoid publishing private school information, addresses, contact details, IDs or other unnecessary personal information.
